# SEO strategy – BAME KANG & Co
Research date: 24 September 2026. Sources: Google autocomplete (live query data for Cameroon,
English and French), Google/Bing search results for the main commercial terms, and the public
websites of competing Cameroonian firms.

**Honest note on data:** search *volume* numbers require a paid tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Google Keyword
Planner inside an Ads account). Everything below is based on free, verifiable signals: Google's own
autocomplete (it only suggests queries people actually type), who currently ranks, and what
competitors publish. Where a term shows no autocomplete variants it means low volume — usually also
low competition, which can still be worth writing for.

**3. OHADA** – strong informational demand, mostly English:
`ohada law`, `ohada law in cameroon`, `ohada law countries`, `ohada law meaning`, `ohada business law`,

**4. Employment / labour**: `cameroon labour law`, `cameroon employment law`, `cameroon labour law code`,

**5. Educational / explanatory** (brings authority and AI citations):
`sources of law in cameroon`, `law in cameroon`, `how to become a lawyer in cameroon`,
`first female lawyer in cameroon`. → New "Cameroon law explained" group answers the first three.

but almost nobody is writing for them. The site now answers all three; these are the queries most
likely to be quoted verbatim by an AI assistant.

## 3. Backlink plan (in priority order)

**Free and verified as accepting listings:**
1. **Google Business Profile** – not a link, but the single biggest ranking factor locally.
2. **Lawzana** (lawzana.com) – free "forever" firm profile; already ranks for "best lawyers in Cameroon/Douala".
3. **HG.org** (hg.org/lawfirms/cameroon) – free law-firm directory, long-established.
4. **Lawyers.com / Martindale** – Douala listings exist for corporate and international law.
5. **Global Law Experts**, **LEX Africa** country pages – Africa-focused legal directories.
6. **Bing Places** and **Apple Business Connect** – the other two map ecosystems.

**Editorial (highest value, needs writing):**
7. **Mondaq** and **Lexology** – both publish Cameroon/OHADA articles from local firms and are read by
   international counsel. Republishing your guides there earns strong links and referral work.
8. **Chambers and Partners** and **The Legal 500** – free submissions each year; the ranked Cameroon
   firms above all use them. This is how foreign firms pick local counsel.
9. **Primerus / Lex Africa / international networks** – membership, paid but referral-generating.

**Local authority:**
10. Cameroon Bar Association directory entry; GICAM and bilateral chamber member pages;
    university guest lectures; quotes in Cameroonian business press on new laws.

**Never:** bought links, PBNs, comment spam. Google devalues them and a law firm's reputation is the product.

## 4. Content optimisation already applied
- Titles and meta descriptions rewritten around the exact phrasings found above.
- 58 FAQs, each a self-contained answer with `FAQPage` structured data (58 `Question` entities on `/faq`).
- Question-shaped H2s inside service pages ("Is this your situation?").
- Internal linking: every guide links to its related services and back.
- `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` expose the whole site as clean text for AI assistants.
- Article/Service/LegalService/Person/Breadcrumb structured data on every page.

## 5. Measuring it
Check monthly in Search Console: impressions and average position for `lawyer douala`, `land title cameroon`,
`register company cameroon`, `ohada`. In GA4 watch `contact_call`, `contact_whatsapp` and `generate_lead`.
Ask ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity "Who are good corporate lawyers in Douala?" once a month and note whether
the firm is named — that is the AI-visibility test.
